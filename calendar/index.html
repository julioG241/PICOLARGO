Days of the Week

A week has seven days. In our calendar, Sunday is the first day of the week and Saturday is the seventh day.

Months and Ordinal Numbers

There are twelve months in a year. January is the first month and December is the twelfth month. We use ordinal numbers for dates: May 10th is read “May tenth”.
